# Proposed investment account groups and SimpleFIN reconciliation

Status: design specification, not implemented or deployed. The existing investment MCP bulk-import commit b0f1bf1 is an ancestor of dev. This design extends the native investment and bank ledgers rather than adding a second financial ledger.

## Goals and non-goals

Connect an existing SimpleFIN-backed native bank Account to one brokerage group that owns one or multiple native Portfolios. Support a combined provider account covering multiple brokerage subaccounts (e.g. taxable, Roth IRA, traditional IRA). Support separate provider accounts and groups without providers. Keep individual portfolio trades, holdings, cash, and performance; treat a provider balance as dated valuation evidence for its explicit scope, not a cash deposit, quantity observation or stock execution. No inferred share quantities, fabricated prices, arbitrary split of combined balances or automated brokerage orders.

## Group, mapping and evidence model

Proposed normalized tables (names illustrative):
- InvestmentAccountGroups: owner, name, presentation settings, timestamps.
- InvestmentAccountGroupMembers: group ID, portfolio ID; unique owner/portfolio membership, with explicit reassignment.
- InvestmentAccountGroupSources: group ID, native account ID, coverage kind (whole group or specific member), effective dates and active state. A native provider account must not cover two overlapping scopes. Reference the existing native account and its stable provider account identity; never store another SimpleFIN credential or connection token. Preserve mappings across reconnect only when identity is proven; otherwise require re-confirmation.
- InvestmentFundingReconciliation: canonical evidence references, scope, posted date, original currency and amount, state, allocation/linked transfer references, provenance, review reason and resolution audit. Pending/unassigned evidence is not a PortfolioTransfer and does not mutate cash or holdings. One canonical event can have multiple source references. Unique source identity includes owner/provider connection/account/posted transaction ID or native source record ID, with explicit supersession rather than deleting audit history.
- Trade-coverage reviews reference actual funding, settlement and broker evidence; optional allocations from funding to trades are explanatory, not new cash movements. Do not require each stock purchase to be assigned to an individual contribution: cash is fungible.

Reuse native account-group UI patterns and existing portfolio visibility independently of aggregation ownership. All operations must enforce ownership of the group, source account, portfolios, evidence, transfers and trades. Validate currencies, dates, active mappings and authorized scope; do not match solely by institution name or amount.

## Source authority and net worth

Provider combined balance is authoritative for the mapped scope while it is the chosen supported valuation. A native reconstruction is a comparison, not additional assets. Example: provider total $25,000; three child reconstructed values $15,000/$8,000/$2,000; aggregate $25,000. If children total $24,600, display a $400 reconciliation difference, not an invented cash allocation. Show source, effective observation dates and coverage; do not compare differing valuation dates as if simultaneous. Do not use available-balance as investable cash unless provider semantics explicitly prove that.

Implement one coverage/authority resolver shared by current net worth, combined balance history, historical personal net worth, net-worth drivers and investment analytics group views. Existing Personal Finance reporting account links, the pre-Nov-5 historical source cutoff, explicit exclusions, disabled mirror portfolios and historical aggregate evidence must be respected. No automatic enabling of excluded portfolios. Count each covered identity once; a combined historical observation cannot be disaggregated into children without evidence. Nonoverlapping unmapped members may contribute independently. If chosen provider source is missing or stale, expose its last actual date; reconstructed fallback requires verified complete same-scope data and explicit source policy. Partial data remains partial, not zero or silently filled. Group membership is presentation/coverage, not itself a financial record. Do not sum provider valuation with stock/cash, unassigned funding, or cash already included in the group.

## Sync and reconciliation

Run after the existing successful posted SimpleFIN transaction persistence, using stored transactions and actual provider timestamps. No additional provider requests; preserve the request budget, checkpoints, overlap, deduplication and error semantics in SIMPLEFIN-SYNC.md. A bounded reconciliation job reads newly persisted/changed rows and can explicitly backfill a selected date range; use transactional locks and unique source-reference constraints for retry and concurrent sync/import safety. Pending, reversed, failed or unsupported rows are not silently treated as funding; updated/deleted evidence reopens review. Retain source-specific errors. Avoid triggering full investment analytics in the sync request path.

Classify evidence into external contribution, withdrawal, internal brokerage transfer, security settlement, fee, dividend/interest, adjustment or unknown using explicit provider metadata, existing confirmed rules or reviewed evidence. A balance change alone is never a contribution. If the source checking expense and destination SimpleFIN credit match, attach both to one funding event; use stable source IDs first and otherwise require same amount/currency, compatible direction, verified account mapping and a bounded posting window. Multiple candidates or split/combined mismatches require review; two independent equal deposits must remain separate. Reuse principal matching patterns in principal-matching.ts but do not write duplicate PersonalFinanceCashFlows. Where principal/Sheet reconciliation already describes the event, reference it instead of re-applying it.

Allocation:
- Unique, verified destination (or confirmed source-specific rule): route to that portfolio; do not use name matching alone.
- Combined account with multiple children and no destination evidence: retain unassigned group-level funding and prompt for destination. Do not add the same deposit to every portfolio or allocate by relative holdings. The group may display confirmed unassigned external funding separately; the native portfolio contribution view counts only assigned transfers until group-aware aggregation is implemented.
- User can split a deposit across children only with explicit allocations conserving original amount/currency; require atomic resolution. Existing link service currently has one transactionId per transfer; extend its model/association deliberately before supporting multi-allocation. Do not create synthetic bank debits.
- For an unlinked single source bank transaction, reuse linkTransactionToPortfolio (expense means inbound, income means outbound). Existing service creates a PortfolioTransfer, sets transferNature, and changes cash only if affectsCash=true. It rejects already-linked/common transfers. Extend the canonical services for attaching a second bank leg or associating an existing broker-imported PortfolioTransfer without creating a new transfer, including already matched bank-to-bank transfers; do not bypass those guards or manually mutate balances. Source/destination transfer classifications must keep both ledger legs out of spending/income reports.
- If existing native cash already contains the verified historical funding, use affectsCash=false only with explicit evidence and existing service validation. If an existing native transfer already represents the event, link evidence to it; affectsCash=false is NOT a way to create a duplicate contribution.
- Reconcile incoming broker CSV/MCP events against the same canonical event before invoking native transfer creation. Existing sourceRowId prevents same-source repeats, not cross-source duplicates. Reuse locks, provenance and canonical investment transaction services for cash/holdings changes.
- Reversals, unlink/delete, reallocation and broker corrections require audit and a consistent reversal/reassignment, never an extra net deposit. Recompute dependent models only after a successful commit, using existing source revisions and worker scheduling.

## Missing investment activity review

A $200 external deposit is not proof of a $200 stock purchase. On verified assignment the native transfer increases cash once; cash can remain deliberately uninvested. If later broker evidence indicates $120 of buys and $80 of buys (or actual fees/cash remaining), import the executions through canonical investment transaction services. Cash falls by verified settlements while holdings increase by verified quantities. Deposit stays $200; purchase cost is not a new deposit or expense. If a provider reports only a $200 debit with no execution details, create an unmatched settlement review, not a guessed trade. With insufficient evidence, offer review after a configurable settlement grace period only as a possible missing activity, not a confirmed error.

Reconciliation center states: unassigned destination, possible duplicate, unmatched settlement, funding awaiting trade evidence, intentionally held cash, reconciled, balance discrepancy, unsupported corporate activity. Preserve partial coverage when the provider feed has no trades. Show residual and source evidence; a deposit can fund several buys or remain cash, a buy can use older cash/dividends/sale proceeds, and one combined brokerage transaction may represent several executions. Fees, withdrawals, reinvestments, corporate actions, and foreign currency settlement must retain their distinct meanings. Do not auto-create trades based on changes in market value or on arbitrary dollar matching.

## UI

Investments -> Groups: list institution/group name, linked native provider account(s), member portfolios, source balance/as-of timestamp, reconstructed value and coverage, reconciliation difference when comparable, known cash and unassigned funding. Group filters reuse existing portfolio scopes without introducing new calculation variants unnecessarily. Existing native account/portfolio pages remain accessible.

Investments -> Reconciliation: source transaction, date, amount, suggested group/member, existing evidence and explanation. Actions: assign portfolio, split with conservation, link existing contribution/trades, import CSV, add actual trade, mark cash retained, classify non-funding, snooze, and resolve. Preview cash and net-worth effects before applying. No auto-link on a mere guess. A nonblocking login reminder, following the recovery-email onboarding pattern, shows outstanding actionable review count after authentication/initial sync (not a blocking login requirement); Open review, Remind later, Dismiss resolved. Per-user snooze, rate limit to avoid repeated prompts, and dashboard badge. Do not display sensitive amounts in generic login prompts or outside the authenticated app.

## API and MCP

Authenticated web APIs for group list/create/update/delete, member and provider mapping management, read-only reconciliation list, preview and explicit atomic resolve actions. Keep read and mutation permissions distinct. MCP should expose corresponding read tools and scoped write tools for assignment and linking; reuse investment MCP read/write scopes and deletion permissions where applicable. Never disclose provider credentials, or let MCP choose arbitrary portfolios on ambiguous evidence. Bulk trade imports use stable broker IDs and canonical existing services; return per-row duplicate/review/result status and avoid silently retrying ambiguous writes. Verify tools/list using a freshly authorized connection after deployment; repository presence does not establish ChatGPT discovery.

## Implementation order and verification

1. Add group/membership/source/evidence schema and owner-scoped APIs; read-only grouping UI. Existing deployments have no mappings by default. No balance behavior changes before explicit mapping and review.
2. Add bounded posted-transaction reconciliation in preview/dry-run mode. Validate actual broker and bank fixtures, cross-source matches, same-dollar duplicates, pending/reversal cases, one-to-many accounts and stable reconnect identities.
3. Implement atomic canonical funding assignment/linkage, cash-affecting and historical-only cases, evidence associations and reversal behavior. Test races between sync, CSV and MCP, and confirm spending, cash and funding are changed exactly once.
4. Integrate shared net-worth coverage resolver with historical cutoff/source precedence and all current/historical aggregate consumers. Verify before/after preservation fingerprints and totals for a mapped one-to-one account and a one-to-many group, partial coverage, provider outages and excluded mirror portfolios.
5. Add missing-trade review and login reminder; explicit snooze and cash-retained resolution. Verify no false required purchases and cash/trade settlement correctness.
6. Add scoped MCP discovery and actions, then verify test suite, migration/rollback, production builds, deployed API and reconnect discovery. Do not enable automatic financial writes until preview and validation have passed.

Rollout must preserve original financial records, snapshots, existing provider budget, OAuth scopes and backups. No inferred transactions or retroactive auto-allocation during migration. Use synthetic fixtures and private operator-reviewed comparisons, not committed real balances or credentials.

## Detailed M1 CSV import and review contract

The detailed M1 file includes Date, Posted Date, Symbol, Description, Transaction Type, Amount, Units, Unit Type, Unit Price, Security Id and Security Id Type; it does not include an account ID or a unique execution ID. User must select the actual member portfolio (or a previously verified source mapping), never assume the combined SimpleFIN group is the CSV account. Preserve original CSV privately and retain normalized source rows with file hash, occurrence and date evidence. The new parser recognizes this format; the integration below is still proposed.

- Preview imports across the full file, not only since the newest existing date; older missing trades and corrections may be included. Exact stable source identities already applied are skipped. Legacy summary/import records with different IDs must be reconciled cross-source rather than replayed. For previously imported trades with different source keys require compatible portfolio, security identity, execution date, direction, quantity, settlement amount and currency; ambiguous or rounded legacy matches require review.
- Each completed ACH row is first matched against canonical funding, bank legs, Personal Finance principal evidence, and existing native portfolio transfers. If exactly one compatible event exists, attach evidence and resolve destination if verified; do not add another cash movement. If the funding was previously assigned, preserve it; mismatched destination or amount is review. Unmatched CSV funding is a candidate for one new canonical event only after review. A full or overlapping export with two identical rows must retain two occurrences, but a partial export cannot establish which occurrence it represents without further evidence. Cross-source matching must be one-to-one and conserve amount; no fuzzy auto-linking in an ambiguous set.
- Import only missing executed buys/sells via native investment services and the canonical source ID. Preserve the exact settlement amount rather than recomputing from rounded quantities and prices. Associate trades to settlement evidence or plausible deposit batches for explanation, not as extra contributions. Deposit-to-trade comparison allows dates to differ and residual cash/dividends/fees; equal amount alone is not proof. Reconcile unmatched settlements and missing-trade reviews only when supported, never clear them solely because a CSV was uploaded. Returned ACH deposits, dividend reversals, margin interest, fees and bonuses are separately classified/reviewed, not external contributions.
- Preview shows already imported, matched existing funding, new trades, new funding candidates, ambiguous matches, reversals/unsupported rows, and unresolved reviews. Explicit confirm uses atomic canonical services and unique evidence IDs, revalidates preview against current revisions, and reports per-event results. Repeat import makes zero additional cash/holdings changes for previously applied evidence. Reordering and overlapping full-day exports preserve identity; do not commit private CSV or actual values to source control.
