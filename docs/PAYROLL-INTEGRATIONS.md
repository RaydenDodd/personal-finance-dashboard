# Read-only payroll integration review

Reviewed November 19, 2026. No provider was purchased, activated, or given payroll credentials. Native manual entry, CSV imports and local text-PDF parsing remain the inexpensive default. Bank deposits from SimpleFIN establish received net money; they do not establish salary, gross wages, deductions, or employer contributions.

## Decision

Use the existing SimpleFIN connection for bank deposits and reconcile them with locally imported/manual paychecks. A salary entry is a dated planning assumption, not proof of received gross pay. Keep API adapters disabled until real production access, price, data retention, read-only permissions and supported payroll platform are confirmed. There is no need for a new paid subscription or publicly exposed webhook just to support the current manual/import workflow.

If a payroll connector is added later, require explicit consent and revocation, restrict to income/paystub/employment read endpoints, encrypt provider credentials, retain source IDs for deduplication, record effective dates and source provenance, and avoid duplicating bank net deposits. Never automate payroll login or change deposit destinations.
